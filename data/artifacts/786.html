Formulas

Snell's law: n₁ sin θ₁ = n₂ sin θ₂ Critical angle: θc = sin⁻¹(n₂ / n₁)   (only when n₁ > n₂) Mirror / lens formula: 1/f = 1/v − 1/u   (lens)  |  1/f = 1/v + 1/u (mirror) Magnification: m = h′/h = −v/u (mirror)  |  m = v/u (lens)

Quick Quiz

1. Light passes from glass (n = 1.5) into air (n = 1.0). What happens beyond the critical angle?

Beyond θc the ray cannot leave the denser medium, so it is totally internally reflected.

2. An object is placed beyond the centre of curvature of a concave mirror. The image is:

For u beyond C, the real inverted image forms between F and C and is diminished.

3. A convex lens always forms which image for an object placed closer than its focal length?

Inside the focal length a convex lens acts as a magnifier: image is virtual, erect and enlarged.

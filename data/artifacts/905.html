Divide a segment in ratio m : n

Parallel-lines (ray) method: draw an acute ray, mark m+n equal steps, join the last to B, and draw a parallel through the m-th point.

Step 0
Set m and n, then press Next step.
Why it works: The equal steps on the ray cut off equal segments; the parallel line through the m-th point cuts AB in the same ratio (Basic Proportionality / Thales theorem), so AP : PB = m : n exactly.

Tangents to a circle from an external point

Join centre O to external point P, find the midpoint M of OP, draw the circle on OP as diameter — it meets the given circle at the tangent points.

Step 0
Press Next step to construct both tangents.
Why it works: Any angle in a semicircle is 90° (Thales). Since T lies on the circle with diameter OP, angle OTP = 90°, so OT ⟂ PT. A radius perpendicular to a line at a circle point means that line is a tangent — hence PT touches the circle.

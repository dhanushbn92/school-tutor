Nutrient Reference & Energy Note

NutrientMain functionEnergy (kcal/g)
Energy note: Carbohydrates and proteins yield ~4 kcal/g, fats ~9 kcal/g; vitamins, minerals and water yield no energy but are essential regulators. Fibre (roughly indigestible) adds bulk, aids bowel movement and gives negligible energy. An average adult needs about 2000–2500 kcal/day depending on age, sex and activity.

Malnutrition, Obesity & Healthy Living

Under-nutrition

Too little energy/protein: leads to marasmus (energy + protein deficit, severe wasting) and kwashiorkor (protein deficit with oedema). Signs: low weight, stunting, weakness, poor immunity.

Over-nutrition & Obesity

Excess energy stored as fat (BMI ≥ 30). Raises risk of type-2 diabetes, hypertension, heart disease and joint problems. Driven by high-calorie diets and low activity.

Healthy-lifestyle tips: eat a balanced varied diet, prefer whole grains, fruits & vegetables; limit sugar, salt and trans-fats; drink adequate water; exercise ≥30 min/day; maintain hygiene; get adequate sleep; avoid tobacco and alcohol.
Food adulteration: deliberate lowering of food quality by adding cheap/harmful substances (e.g. chalk in flour, water/starch in milk, argemone oil in mustard oil, metanil-yellow dye in turmeric). It reduces nutritive value and can cause poisoning. Laws such as the FSSAI standards and simple home tests help detect it.

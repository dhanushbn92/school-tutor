Expanding Universe — Hubble's Law

Recession velocity v = H0 × d
H0 (Hubble constant)70 km/s per Mpc
Fraction of light speed
Farther galaxies recede faster — space itself stretches, so every observer sees others moving away. This uniform expansion is the evidence for the Big Bang.

Key Facts

Light-year (ly)

Distance light travels in one year in vacuum ≈ 9.46 × 1012 km. Light speed c ≈ 3 × 105 km/s.

Parsec (pc)

Distance at which 1 AU subtends 1 arc-second ≈ 3.26 light-years ≈ 3.086 × 1013 km.

Astronomical Unit (AU)

Mean Earth–Sun distance ≈ 1.496 × 108 km. Used for Solar-System scales.

Hubble's Law

v = H0d. Recession speed is proportional to distance; H0 ≈ 70 km/s/Mpc.

Redshift

Light from receding galaxies is stretched to longer (redder) wavelengths — direct proof of expansion.

Observable Universe

Radius ≈ 46 billion light-years; it holds an estimated 2 trillion galaxies.

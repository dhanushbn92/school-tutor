eˣ is its own derivative

y = f(x) f'(x) (derivative) tangent at x = a
Set the base to e and the orange derivative curve sits exactly on the teal curve — slope of the tangent equals the height of the point. For a general base, the derivative is stretched by ln a.

Logarithm derivatives & the inverse reflection

y = logₐx derivative 1/(x ln a) y = aˣ (inverse) line y = x
y = aˣ and y = logₐx are mirror images across the dashed line y = x. The natural log is special: d/dx(ln x) = 1/x exactly (no ln-factor).

Chain rule & logarithmic differentiation

y = f(x) f'(x) exact formula numerical derivative (dots)
The purple dots are computed by the difference quotient [f(x+h) − f(x−h)] / 2h with h = 0.001. They land on the orange exact-derivative curve — a live sanity check.

Facts panel — key derivatives

d/dx (eˣ)= eˣ
d/dx (aˣ), a > 0= aˣ · ln a
d/dx (ln x), x > 0= 1 / x
d/dx (logₐx)= 1 / (x · ln a)
d/dx (e^(g(x)))= e^(g(x)) · g'(x)
d/dx (ln(g(x)))= g'(x) / g(x)
Log differentiationln y = ln f ⇒ y'/y = (ln f)'
Use logarithmic differentiation whenever the variable appears in both base and exponent (like xˣ) or for long products/quotients: take ln of both sides, differentiate, then multiply by y.

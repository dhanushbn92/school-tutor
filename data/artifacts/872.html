Key facts

Standard equation(x−h)² + (y−k)² = r², centre (h,k), radius r
General equationx² + y² + 2gx + 2fy + c = 0
Centre from general form(−g, −f)
Radius from general form√(g² + f² − c) (real circle needs g²+f²−c > 0)
Point (x₁,y₁) positionS₁ = (x₁−h)²+(y₁−k)²−r² : <0 inside, =0 on, >0 outside
Tangent condition (line to circle)perpendicular distance from centre = r
Tangent from y=mx+c to x²+y²=a²c² = a²(1+m²)

Quick quiz

Q1. Centre & radius of x²+y²−6x+4y−12=0?
a) (3,−2), r=5   b) (−3,2), r=5   c) (3,−2), r=25   d) (6,−4), r=5
a) (3,−2), r=5. 2g=−6→g=−3, 2f=4→f=2, so centre (−g,−f)=(3,−2); r=√(9+4+12)=√25=5.
Q2. The line 3x+4y=10 and circle x²+y²=4 — relationship?
a) secant   b) tangent   c) does not meet
b) tangent. Distance of centre (0,0) from line = |−10|/√(9+16)=10/5=2 = radius. Distance = r ⇒ tangent.
Q3. Is the point (1,2) inside x²+y²=9?
a) inside   b) on   c) outside
a) inside. S₁ = 1²+2²−9 = 5−9 = −4 < 0, so the point lies inside the circle.

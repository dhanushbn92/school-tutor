Key facts

  • First principles: f′(x) = limh→0 [f(x+h) − f(x)] / h — the limit of the secant slope.
  • Power rule: d/dx (xⁿ) = n·xn−1 for any real n.
  • Constant multiple & sum: d/dx [a·f ± b·g] = a·f′ ± b·g′.
  • Product rule: (uv)′ = u′v + uv′.
  • Quotient rule: (u/v)′ = (u′v − uv′) / v².
  • Chain rule: d/dx f(g(x)) = f′(g(x))·g′(x) — "derivative of outer × derivative of inner".

Quick quiz

Q1. If f(x) = x³, what is f′(2)?
f′(x) = 3x². So f′(2) = 3·(2²) = 12.
Q2. At a local maximum of a smooth curve, the derivative f′ is…
…equal to 0. The tangent is horizontal; f′ changes from positive (rising) to negative (falling).
Q3. Differentiate y = (2x + 1)⁵ using the chain rule.
Outer u⁵ → 5u⁴; inner (2x+1) → 2. So dy/dx = 5(2x+1)⁴·2 = 10(2x + 1)⁴.

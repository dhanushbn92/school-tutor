Key Facts

1st Law (Inertia)

A body stays at rest, or moves with uniform velocity in a straight line, unless an unbalanced (net) external force acts on it.

2nd Law

Rate of change of momentum ∝ applied force. In everyday form F = m a. Also F = Δp/Δt.

3rd Law

To every action there is an equal and opposite reaction. The pair acts on two different bodies.

Momentum

p = m v (unit kg·m/s). Total momentum of an isolated system is conserved.

Impulse

J = F Δt = Δp (unit N·s). A small force over a long time can equal a large force over a short time.

Units

Force: 1 newton (N) = 1 kg·m·s⁻² — the force that gives a 1 kg mass an acceleration of 1 m/s².

Key facts

Parts of a circle
  • Centre — fixed point O, equidistant from all points on the circle.
  • Radius (r) — segment from centre to the circle.
  • Diameter (d = 2r) — chord through the centre; the longest chord.
  • Chord — segment joining any two points on the circle.
  • Arc — part of the circle between two points; the shorter is the minor arc, the longer the major arc.
  • Circumference — the whole boundary (perimeter) of the circle.
  • Sector — region bounded by two radii and an arc.
  • Segment — region bounded by a chord and its arc.
Chord properties
  • The perpendicular from the centre to a chord bisects the chord (and vice versa).
  • Equal chords of a circle are equidistant from the centre.
  • Chords equidistant from the centre are equal in length.
  • If half-chord = a and distance from centre = d, then r² = a² + d², so a longer chord lies closer to the centre.
Circumference & area

Circumference = 2πr = πd

Area = πr²  (π ≈ 3.14159)

Arc length & sector area

For a central angle θ (degrees):

Arc length = (θ/360) × 2πr

Sector area = (θ/360) × πr²

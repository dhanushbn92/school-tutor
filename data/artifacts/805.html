Key facts & formulae

  • Distance — total length of the path travelled. Scalar (only magnitude).
  • Displacement — shortest straight line from start to end, with direction. Vector.
  • Speed = distance ÷ time. Scalar. Velocity = displacement ÷ time. Vector.
  • Acceleration = rate of change of velocity = (v − u) ÷ t.
  • Uniform motion — equal distances in equal time intervals (constant velocity, a = 0).
  • Non-uniform motion — velocity changes with time (a ≠ 0).

Three equations of motion (uniform acceleration)

First
v = u + at
Second
s = ut + ½at²
Third
v² = u² + 2as

Symbols & SI units

u = initial velocity · m/s v = final velocity · m/s a = acceleration · m/s² s = displacement · m t = time · s

Graph clues

  • On a position–time graph the slope = velocity. Straight line ⇒ uniform motion; curve ⇒ acceleration.
  • On a velocity–time graph the slope = acceleration and the area under the line = displacement.

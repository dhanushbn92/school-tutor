Geometric meaning (2 × 2)

|det| equals the area of the parallelogram spanned by the two column vectors u = (a, c) and v = (b, d).

Cramer's rule — solve a linear system

Replace a column of the coefficient matrix by the constants to get Δx, Δy (Δz).

Area of a triangle

From vertices A(x₁,y₁), B(x₂,y₂), C(x₃,y₃): Area = ½ |x₁(y₂−y₃) + x₂(y₃−y₁) + x₃(y₁−y₂)|.

Properties (quick note)

Row/column operations
• Swapping two rows (or columns) flips the sign of the determinant.
• Multiplying one row by k multiplies det by k.
• Adding a multiple of one row to another leaves det unchanged.
• If two rows (or columns) are equal (or proportional), det = 0.
• det(Aᵀ) = det(A); det(AB) = det(A)·det(B).

Key facts

2 × 2 determinant
|A| = a·d − b·c for A = [[a, b], [c, d]].
3 × 3 by cofactor expansion (Row 1)
|A| = a₁₁M₁₁ − a₁₂M₁₂ + a₁₃M₁₃
where each minor M is the 2×2 determinant left after deleting that entry's row & column.

Cramer's rule
For AX = B with Δ = det(A) ≠ 0: x = Δx/Δ, y = Δy/Δ, z = Δz/Δ, where Δx replaces the x-column of A by B, and so on. If Δ = 0 the system has no unique solution.
Triangle area & collinearity
Area = ½|det| with rows (x₁ y₁ 1),(x₂ y₂ 1),(x₃ y₃ 1). Area = 0 ⇒ the three points are collinear.

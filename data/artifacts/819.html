Sexual Reproduction — two parents, gametes, variation

Why variation matters

Two parents each donate a haploid gamete (male + female). Their fusion (fertilisation) makes a zygote that mixes both sets of genes.

This variation helps a species adapt and survive changing environments — the key advantage over asexual reproduction.

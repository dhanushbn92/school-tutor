Secant vs Tangent

Drag the line handle up/down. A line that cuts the circle at 2 points is a secant; touching at exactly 1 point is a tangent; otherwise it misses. At the tangent, watch the radius meet the line at a right angle.

Two Tangents from an External Point

Drag point P outside the circle. The two tangents from P are drawn to their points of contact. They are equal in length, and line PO bisects the angle between them.

Tangent Length = √(d² − r²)

Drag P. Triangle O–T–P is right-angled at the point of contact T. So PT = √(d² − r²), where d = OP and r = radius. Slide the radius too.

Alternate segment: the angle between a tangent and a chord drawn from the point of contact equals the inscribed angle in the alternate segment.

Key Facts

  • Tangent ⟂ radius: the tangent at any point of a circle is perpendicular to the radius drawn to the point of contact.
  • Equal tangents: the two tangents drawn from an external point to a circle are equal in length.
  • Angle bisected: the line joining the external point to the centre bisects the angle between the two tangents.
  • Tangent length: from a point at distance d from the centre, tangent length = √(d² − r²).
  • Alternate segment: tangent–chord angle = inscribed angle in the alternate segment.

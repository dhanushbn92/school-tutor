Key Formulas

F = k · q₁q₂ / r²
Coulomb's law. k = 9 × 10⁹ N·m²/C². Force is along the line joining the charges: repulsive for like charges, attractive for unlike.
E = F / q₀
Electric field = force per unit positive test charge q₀. Unit: newton per coulomb (N/C) or volt per metre (V/m). E is a vector.
E = k · Q / r²
Field due to a point charge Q at distance r. Points away from +Q and toward −Q.
Enet = Σ Ei
Superposition principle: the field of several charges is the vector sum of each charge's field, computed independently.

Field-line rules

  • Lines start on positive charges and end on negative charges (or at infinity).
  • The tangent to a line gives the direction of E at that point.
  • Line density is proportional to field strength — closer lines mean a stronger field.
  • Field lines never cross (E has one unique direction at each point).

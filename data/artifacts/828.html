Introduction to Classification

Tap a rank to see how the honey bee is classified. Ranks go broad (top) to specific (bottom).

Taxonomy groups organisms by shared features. Broader ranks contain more, less-similar organisms.

Kingdoms & Domains

Tap a group for its distinguishing features.

Binomial nomenclature (Carl Linnaeus, 1758): every species gets a two-word Latin name - Genus species. Genus is capitalised, species is lower-case, both italicised (or underlined when handwritten). Example: the honey bee is Apis mellifera; humans are Homo sapiens.

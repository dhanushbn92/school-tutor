Mass–energy & binding energy per nucleon

Peak near Fe-56 (~8.8 MeV/nucleon). Light nuclei gain by fusing toward the peak; heavy nuclei gain by splitting toward it — both move to more tightly bound (higher B.E./A) states, releasing energy.

E = m c²  ·  1 u = 931.5 MeV/c² Binding energy = (mass of separate nucleons − mass of nucleus) × c². Higher B.E./A ⇒ more stable.

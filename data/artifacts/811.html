Key Facts

Ohm's law
V = I × R
Voltage (V) equals current (A) times resistance (Ω), at constant temperature.
Series circuit
Same current everywhere; resistances add: R = R₁+R₂. One bulb fails → both go off.
Parallel circuit
Same voltage across each; 1/R = 1/R₁+1/R₂. One bulb fails → other keeps glowing.
Electric power
P = V×I = I²R = V²/R, measured in watts (W).
Energy & the bill
E = P × t. Commercial unit = kilowatt-hour (kWh). 1 kWh = 3.6×10⁶ J.
Heating effect
Joule's law: H = I²Rt. Basis of heaters, geysers and fuses.

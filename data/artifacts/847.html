    Contraception & Reproductive Health

    Educational overview of the main categories of contraceptive methods. Tap a category for how it works.

    CategoryPrinciple
    BarrierPhysically blocks sperm from reaching the egg (condom, diaphragm)
    HormonalAlters hormones to prevent ovulation (oral pills, injectables)
    Intra-uterine (IUD)Device in the uterus prevents implantation/fertilisation
    SurgicalPermanent — vasectomy (male), tubectomy (female)
    NaturalRhythm/periodic abstinence based on the fertile window
    Clinical note: This is general educational information for the NIOS syllabus, not personalised medical advice. Decisions about contraception and reproductive health should be made with a qualified healthcare professional.

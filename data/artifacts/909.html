The two special triangles

Cut a unit square along its diagonal → an isosceles right triangle with legs 1, 1 and hypotenuse √2. That gives 45°.

Cut an equilateral triangle of side 2 down its height → a triangle with sides 1, √3, 2 and angles 30°, 60°, 90°.

tan θ = sin θ / cos θ
tan 90° is undefined (cos 90° = 0)
sin θ = cos(90°−θ)

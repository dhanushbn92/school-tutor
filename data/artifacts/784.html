Key formulas

Field of a long straight wire at distance r:

B = μ₀ I / (2π r)

Field at centre of a circular loop (radius R, N turns):

B = μ₀ N I / (2R)

Uniform field inside a long solenoid:

B = μ₀ n I (n = turns per metre)

Force on a current-carrying wire:

F = B I L sinθ

Force on a moving charge (magnitude, v ⟂ B):

F = q v B

Radius of the circular path:

r = m v / (q B)

μ₀ = 4π × 10⁻⁷ T·m/A (permeability of free space)

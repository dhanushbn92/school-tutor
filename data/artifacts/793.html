Facts & Formulas

  • Diode: conducts only when forward-biased; used to convert AC to DC (rectification).
  • Half-wave rectifier: one diode, output only on positive half-cycles. Ripple frequency = supply frequency (50 Hz).
  • Full-wave (bridge): four diodes, both half-cycles used. Ripple frequency = 2 × supply = 100 Hz.
  • Smoothing capacitor: charges at peaks, discharges through the load, reducing ripple toward steady DC.
  • Transistor current gain: β = IC / IB, so IC = β × IB.
  • Regions: Cutoff (IB≈0, OFF) · Active (linear amplification) · Saturation (fully ON, switch closed).
  • Logic: AND = both 1 · OR = any 1 · NOT = invert · NAND = NOT(AND).

Facts & formulas

Magnitude (2D)
|a| = √(a₁²+a₂²)
Unit vector
â = a / |a|
Dot product
a·b = a₁b₁+a₂b₂ = |a||b|cosθ
Angle
cosθ = a·b / (|a||b|)
Projection of a on b
(a·b)/|b|
Cross product
|a×b| = |a||b|sinθ = area

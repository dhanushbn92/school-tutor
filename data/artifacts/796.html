Lasers — Light Amplification by Stimulated Emission of Radiation

Watch atoms absorb energy, then emit light. A laser needs population inversion (more atoms up than down) so that stimulated emission wins.

ground state excited state emitted photon

Spontaneous: excited atoms drop on their own, emitting photons in random directions — like an ordinary lamp.

Key properties & uses

Monochromatic

Nearly a single wavelength / one colour.

Coherent

Waves march in step (same phase).

Directional

Narrow beam, spreads very little.

Intense

Energy concentrated in a tiny area.

Uses: CD/DVD & barcode readers, fibre-optic communication, laser surgery & eye treatment, cutting & welding, distance measurement (LIDAR), holography.

The Scale Ladder — down to the nanometre

"Nano" means one-billionth. 1 nm = 10⁻⁹ m (a metre split into a billion pieces). Nanotechnology builds and studies matter at 1–100 nm — where materials gain new properties.

Why the nanoscale matters

  • At the nanoscale, a much larger fraction of atoms sit on the surface, changing chemistry and strength.
  • Carbon nanotubes and graphene (one atom thick) are extremely strong and conduct well.
  • Nanoparticles are used in sunscreens, drug delivery, sensors, and stain-resistant fabrics.

Superconductivity — zero resistance below Tc

In a superconductor, electrical resistance drops abruptly to zero when cooled below a critical temperature Tc. Current then flows with no energy loss.

Meissner effect — magnetic levitation

A superconductor expels magnetic field from its interior, so a magnet floats above it. Drag the slider above below Tc to make the magnet lift off.

Other modern frontiers

Particle physics

The Higgs boson (confirmed 2012 at CERN's LHC) explains how fundamental particles get mass.

Quantum computing

Qubits exploit superposition & entanglement to explore many possibilities at once.

Applications of superconductors

MRI scanners, maglev trains, and powerful research magnets.

Standard Model

Our best theory of the known elementary particles and three of the four forces.

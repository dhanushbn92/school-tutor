Quick Quiz

1. In angiosperm double fertilization, the endosperm is:
One male gamete fuses with the egg → zygote (2n); the other fuses with the two polar nuclei of the central cell → primary endosperm nucleus (3n). This triple fusion is unique to angiosperms.
2. A runner, tuber and bulb are examples of:
Runner (strawberry), tuber (potato) and bulb (onion) are natural vegetative organs that give rise to new plants without seeds — offspring are genetically identical clones.
3. Wind-pollinated flowers typically have:
Anemophilous (wind) flowers produce abundant light dry pollen, have exposed anthers and large feathery stigmas, and usually lack showy petals, scent or nectar.

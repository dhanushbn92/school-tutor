Rutherford Alpha Scattering

α-particle (He²⁺) Gold nucleus (+Ze)

What it shows

Most α-particles pass almost straight through — the atom is mostly empty space. A very few pass close to the tiny, dense, positively-charged nucleus and are deflected through large angles; the rarest hit near head-on and bounce back.

Key Facts

Nutrition types

Autotrophic (green plants make food by photosynthesis) and heterotrophic (animals, fungi depend on others). Human nutrition is holozoic.

Digestive enzymes

Salivary amylase (mouth), pepsin + HCl (stomach), and pancreatic + intestinal enzymes plus bile break carbohydrates, proteins and fats into absorbable units.

Double circulation

Blood flows through the heart twice in one cycle: pulmonary circuit (heart↔lungs) and systemic circuit (heart↔body). Keeps oxygenated and deoxygenated blood separate.

Respiration equation

Cellular respiration releases energy stored in glucose.C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + energy (ATP)

Aerobic vs anaerobic

Aerobic (with O₂) fully breaks glucose in mitochondria, releasing much energy. Anaerobic (without O₂) gives lactic acid in muscles or ethanol in yeast, with little energy.

Excretion

Kidneys filter nitrogenous waste (mainly urea) from blood, forming urine: filtration in the glomerulus, then selective reabsorption in the tubule.

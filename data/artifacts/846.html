Auxin made at the shoot tip suppresses lateral (axillary) buds. Remove the tip → auxin drops → laterals grow (bushy plant). This is why gardeners prune/pinch tips.

Tip intact: high apical auxin suppresses side buds → single dominant stem.

Tropisms & Photoperiodism

Quick facts

  • Growth is measured as increase in fresh/dry weight, length, area or cell number — it is irreversible.
  • Meristems (apical, lateral/cambium, intercalary) have cells that keep dividing.
  • Differentiation, dedifferentiation & redifferentiation shape cells and tissues; development = growth + differentiation.
  • The plumule shows negative geotropism (grows up); the radicle shows positive geotropism (grows down).
  • Vernalization = cold treatment that promotes flowering (e.g. winter wheat).

Beyond anchorage and absorption, roots are remodelled for storage, breathing, extra support and more. Explore each above.

Core functions of every root

  • Anchorage: fixes the plant firmly in soil against wind and pull.
  • Absorption: root hairs take up water and dissolved minerals by osmosis and active transport.
  • Conduction: the vascular cylinder passes water up to the stem.
  • Storage: parenchyma of cortex/pith may store food (starch), giving fleshy roots.

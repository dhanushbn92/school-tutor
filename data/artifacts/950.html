मानो हि महतां धनम्

Self-Respect is the Wealth of the Great

NIOS Class 12 Sanskrit · Chapter 16 · मान / स्वाभिमान

कथा · A Story of Self-Respect

An original tale: honour held firm against temptation.

कथा · Story (Devanagari)
एकस्मिन् ग्रामे रमणः नाम दरिद्रः पण्डितः वसति स्म। सः विद्यया समृद्धः किन्तु धनेन निर्धनः आसीत्। तस्मिन् एव ग्रामे एकः धनिकः वणिक् अन्यायेन धनम् अर्जयति स्म। एकदा सः वणिक् पण्डितं प्रति आगत्य अवदत् — “हे पण्डित! जनसमक्षं मम प्रशंसां कुरु, अहं तुभ्यं बहु सुवर्णं दास्यामि।” रमणः स्मितं कृत्वा अवदत् — “हे वणिक्! सुवर्णेन क्षुधा शाम्यति, परं मानभङ्गेन आत्मा म्रियते। असत्यप्रशंसया अहं स्वाभिमानं न विक्रीणामि।” वणिक् लज्जितः अभवत्। रमणः निर्धनः एव अतिष्ठत्, परं तस्य मानः अक्षतः आसीत्। सत्यमेव — मानो हि महतां धनम्।
ekasmin grāme ramaṇaḥ nāma daridraḥ paṇḍitaḥ vasati sma. saḥ vidyayā samṛddhaḥ kintu dhanena nirdhanaḥ āsīt. tasmin eva grāme ekaḥ dhanikaḥ vaṇik anyāyena dhanam arjayati sma. ekadā saḥ vaṇik paṇḍitaṁ prati āgatya avadat — “he paṇḍita! janasamakṣaṁ mama praśaṁsāṁ kuru, ahaṁ tubhyaṁ bahu suvarṇaṁ dāsyāmi.” ramaṇaḥ smitaṁ kṛtvā avadat — “he vaṇik! suvarṇena kṣudhā śāmyati, paraṁ mānabhaṅgena ātmā mriyate. asatyapraśaṁsayā ahaṁ svābhimānaṁ na vikrīṇāmi.” vaṇik lajjitaḥ abhavat. ramaṇaḥ nirdhanaḥ eva atiṣṭhat, paraṁ tasya mānaḥ akṣataḥ āsīt. satyam eva — māno hi mahatāṁ dhanam.
English In a certain village lived a poor scholar named Ramana. He was rich in learning but poor in money. In that same village a wealthy merchant earned his riches by dishonest means. One day the merchant came to the scholar and said, “O scholar! Praise me before the people, and I will give you much gold.” Ramana smiled and replied, “O merchant! Gold quiets hunger, but the loss of honour kills the soul. I will not sell my self-respect for a false praise.” The merchant was ashamed. Ramana remained poor, yet his honour stayed unbroken. Truly — self-respect is the wealth of the great.
शब्दार्थ · Word Meanings

Quick Quiz — tap an option to check

1. For a body in SHM, the acceleration is

a = −ω²x: magnitude is largest at the extremes and points back toward the mean position.

2. If the mass on a spring is quadrupled (×4), the period T

T = 2π√(m/k) ∝ √m. Multiplying m by 4 multiplies √m by 2, so T doubles.

3. At the mean position of an oscillator, the energy is

At x = 0 the speed is greatest, so KE is maximum and PE = ½kx² = 0. Total E is unchanged.

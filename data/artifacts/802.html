Key facts

Mendeleev's Periodic Law (1869): the properties of elements are a periodic function of their atomic masses. He arranged elements in order of increasing atomic mass and left gaps, predicting undiscovered elements (e.g. eka-silicon = germanium).

Modern Periodic Law (Moseley): the properties of elements are a periodic function of their atomic numbers (number of protons). This fixed anomalies in Mendeleev's table.

  • Periods = horizontal rows (7 in total). Period number = number of occupied shells.
  • Groups = vertical columns (18 in total). Elements in a group have the same number of valence electrons and similar properties.
  • Metals sit on the left/centre, non-metals on the upper right, metalloids along the zig-zag staircase between them.

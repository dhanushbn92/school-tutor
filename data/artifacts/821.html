Hard vs Soft Water

Hard water has dissolved Ca²⁺ and Mg²⁺ salts; it wastes soap and forms scale in boilers. Soft water lathers easily. Hardness is removed by boiling (temporary) or using washing soda / ion exchange (permanent).

Key Facts

  • Nitrogen (78%) is the most abundant gas in air; it dilutes oxygen and cycles into proteins via nitrogen fixation.
  • Oxygen (21%) supports respiration and combustion; it is renewed by photosynthesis.
  • Only about 0.04% of air is CO₂, yet it is vital for photosynthesis and traps heat (greenhouse effect).
  • SO₂ and NO₂ dissolve in rainwater to form acid rain, which harms plants, soils and buildings.
  • Water is purified in stages: sedimentation → filtration → chlorination before it reaches taps.

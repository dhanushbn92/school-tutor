Facts & formulas

  • AP — constant difference d. Terms: a, a+d, a+2d, … · nth term aₙ = a + (n−1)d
  • AP sum — Sₙ = n/2·[2a + (n−1)d] = n/2·(a + aₙ)
  • GP — constant ratio r. Terms: a, ar, ar², … · nth term aₙ = a·rⁿ⁻¹
  • GP sum (r ≠ 1) — Sₙ = a(rⁿ − 1)/(r − 1)
  • Infinite GP — converges only when |r| < 1: S∞ = a/(1 − r)
  • Means — Arithmetic mean AM = (x+y)/2 · Geometric mean GM = √(xy) · AM ≥ GM

Quick quiz

1. The 10th term of the AP 3, 7, 11, … is:

a = 3, d = 4 → a₁₀ = 3 + 9·4 = 39.

2. For the GP 1, ½, ¼, … the infinite sum S∞ equals:

a = 1, r = ½, |r| < 1 → S∞ = 1/(1 − ½) = 2.

3. The geometric mean of 4 and 16 is:

GM = √(4·16) = √64 = 8.

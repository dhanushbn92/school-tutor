Photoelectric Effect

Emission
No electrons
Threshold frequency f₀
Max kinetic energy KEₓₓₓ
Stopping potential V₀

Photon energy E = hf. Emission happens only when hf > φ (f > f₀). Raising intensity adds more electrons but never changes their maximum KE — a purely quantum result.

KEmax vs Frequency

Straight line above f₀. Its slope equals Planck's constant h; the x-intercept is the threshold frequency f₀ = φ/h; the y-intercept magnitude is the work function φ.

Photon Picture

Colour
Frequency f = c/λ
Energy E = hf
Momentum p = h/λ

A single photon of light carries both energy (E = hf) and momentum (p = h/λ). Blue/violet photons are more energetic than red ones because they have a higher frequency.

Key Formulas

E = h f
Energy of a photon. h = 6.626×10⁻³⁴ J·s = 4.136×10⁻¹⁵ eV·s.
KEmax = h f − φ
Einstein's photoelectric equation. φ is the work function; emission needs hf > φ.
λ = h / p
de Broglie wavelength of a particle of momentum p = mv.
V₀ = KEmax / e
Stopping potential — the reverse voltage that just halts the fastest electrons.

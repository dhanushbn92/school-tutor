Why atoms bond

  • Octet rule: atoms are most stable with 8 electrons in their outer shell (a full octet). H, He aim for a duet of 2.
  • Why bond? Atoms gain, lose, or share electrons to reach a stable noble-gas configuration — lower energy = more stable.
  • Ionic bond: a metal gives away electrons (becomes +) and a non-metal takes them (becomes −). Opposite charges attract → strong electrostatic bond.
  • Covalent bond: two non-metals share pairs of electrons. One shared pair = single bond, two = double, three = triple.

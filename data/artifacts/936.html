यदि जानासि तद् वद

Yadi Jānāsi Tad Vada — "If you know, then speak"

NIOS Class 12 Sanskrit · Chapter 2 · True Knowledge vs. Empty Boasting

Story: The Boastful Pandit

A learned-sounding scholar is gently humbled by a village boy — read the dialogue, then explore the verses.

A famous पण्डित (pandit) travels village to village boasting of his learning. One evening he meets a simple गोपालकः (cowherd boy). The pandit, proud of his big words, decides to test — and mock — the boy. But the boy answers with quiet, practical wisdom. The theme: यदि जानासि तद् वद — speak only what you truly know; empty boasting is not knowledge.
पण्डितः · The Pandit
बालक! अहं सर्वशास्त्राणि जानामि। किं त्वं किमपि जानासि?
bālaka! ahaṁ sarva-śāstrāṇi jānāmi. kiṁ tvaṁ kim api jānāsi?
"Boy! I know all the scriptures. Do you know anything at all?"
गोपालकः · The Cowherd Boy
आर्य! अहम् अल्पं जानामि। यदि जानामि तदेव वदामि, न मृषा।
ārya! aham alpaṁ jānāmi. yadi jānāmi tad eva vadāmi, na mṛṣā.
"Sir, I know little. What I know, that alone I say — never falsely."
पण्डितः · The Pandit
तर्हि वद — केन विना जीवनं न सम्भवति?
tarhi vada — kena vinā jīvanaṁ na sambhavati?
"Then tell me — without what is life not possible?"
गोपालकः · The Cowherd Boy
जलेन विना, आर्य। जलम् एव जीवनम्। एतत् अहं प्रतिदिनं पश्यामि।
jalena vinā, ārya. jalam eva jīvanam. etat ahaṁ pratidinaṁ paśyāmi.
"Without water, sir. Water itself is life — this I see every day."
पण्डितः → नम्रः · The Pandit, humbled
साधु बालक! तव अल्पं ज्ञानं सत्यम्। मम बहु वचनं तु मदमात्रम्। विद्या विनयं ददाति — इदम् अद्य अवगच्छामि।
sādhu bālaka! tava alpaṁ jñānaṁ satyam. mama bahu vacanaṁ tu mada-mātram. vidyā vinayaṁ dadāti — idam adya avagacchāmi.
"Well said, boy! Your little knowledge is true; my many words were mere pride. Knowledge gives humility — today I understand this."
🪔 Moral of the Dialogue
Real knowledge is shown in truthful, useful speech — not in a flood of proud words. यदि जानासि तद् वद: speak when you truly know, stay humble when you do not.

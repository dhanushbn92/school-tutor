Key facts to remember

  • For y = A·sin(Bx+C)+D: amplitude = |A|, period = 2π/B, phase shift = −C/B, midline y=D.
  • Pythagorean: sin²x + cos²x = 1, 1+tan²x = sec²x, 1+cot²x = cosec²x.
  • Sum: sin(A±B) = sinA cosB ± cosA sinB.
  • Sum: cos(A±B) = cosA cosB ∓ sinA sinB.
  • Double angle: sin2x = 2 sinx cosx, cos2x = 1−2sin²x = 2cos²x−1 = cos²x−sin²x.
  • Tangent: tan2x = 2tanx/(1−tan²x).

Quick quiz — tap to reveal

1. For y = 3·sin(2x) the period is…
a) 2π   b) π   c) 4π   d) π/2
b) π. Period = 2π/B = 2π/2 = π. Amplitude here is 3.
2. sin 2x equals…
a) 2 sin x   b) sin²x − cos²x   c) 2 sin x cos x   d) 1 − 2cos²x
c) 2 sin x cos x — the double-angle identity for sine.
3. If A = 60°, B = 30°, then cos(A+B) = ?
a) 1   b) 0   c) ½   d) √3/2
b) 0. A+B = 90°, and cos 90° = 0. Check: cosAcosB−sinAsinB = ½·(√3/2) − (√3/2)·½ = 0.

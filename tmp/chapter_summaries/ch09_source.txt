=== Chapter 9: Methods of Separation in Everyday Life (id=66) ===
TOPICS:
  - Handpicking Method: Handpicking is a method used to separate solid materials from a mixture based on differences in size, color, and shape. It is done by picking the particles by hand.
  - Threshing And Winnowing: Threshing is the process of beating stalks to separate grains, while winnowing is the method of separating lighter husk from heavier grains by wind or blowing air.
  - Sieving Method: Sieving is a process of separating solids from a mixture based on variations in particle size using a sieve. It allows finer particles to pass through while keeping larger particles behind.
  - Evaporation And Sedimentation: Evaporation is the process of converting a liquid into its vapor, which can be used to separate a solid dissolved in a liquid. Sedimentation is the process of settling down of heavier insoluble components at the bottom of a liquid.
  - Decantation And Filtration: Decantation is the process of removing a liquid from a mixture by tilting the vessel, while filtration is used to separate insoluble solid components from a liquid by passing it through a filter.
  - Churning And Magnetic Separation: Churning is used to extract butter from curd, while magnetic separation is the process of separating magnetic and non-magnetic substances using a magnet.

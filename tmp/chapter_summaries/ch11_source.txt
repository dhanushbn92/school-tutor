=== Chapter 11: Nature's Treasures (id=68) ===
TOPICS:
  - Importance Of Air: Air is essential for our survival, and we need oxygen to perform our bodily functions. The air which we breathe in has oxygen, and most living beings also need oxygen for their survival.
  - Composition Of Air: The air which surrounds the Earth is a mixture of gases, including nitrogen, oxygen, argon, carbon dioxide, and other gases in small quantities. The composition of air is approximately 78% nitrogen, 21% oxygen, and 1% argon, carbon dioxide, and other gases.
  - Water As A Treasure: Water is essential and precious for us, and we need it for many daily activities such as drinking, cooking, bathing, washing, and cleaning. Water covers about two-thirds of the Earth's surface, but most of it is saline or salty and not fit for domestic, agricultural, and industrial use.
  - Energy From The Sun: The Sun is the main source of energy on Earth, and plants get energy from the Sun to produce food. Animals eat plants and grow, and we get food from both plants and animals. The Sun's energy is used for various purposes, including drying, cooking, and generating electricity using solar panels.
  - Forests And Their Importance: Forests are large areas with dense growth of various types of plants and provide food and shelter to many wild animals, including birds and insects. Forests are a natural home for many living beings, and their diversity ensures food for every living being. However, the forest cover has been decreasing due to human activities like large-scale cutting of trees.
  - Soil Rocks And Minerals: Soil is formed by the disintegration of rocks by the actions of the Sun, water, and living organisms over a long time. Soil is a precious treasure that supports biodiversity, and different types of soils are good for growing certain types of plants or making bricks for buildings. Rocks play a vital role in our lives and are used in construction, making tools, and extracting minerals.
  - Fossil Fuels: Fossil fuels are used by most vehicles for transportation, and they come in different types such as petrol and diesel. Rocks are made up of minerals, and important metals like aluminium, gold, copper, and iron are extracted from minerals. Minerals are used in the manufacturing of various products, including airplanes, cars, jewelry, cosmetics, and electrical and electronic equipment.

Right triangle & the six ratios

SOH · CAH · TOA

SOH
sin θ = Opposite / Hypotenuse
CAH
cos θ = Adjacent / Hypotenuse
TOA
tan θ = Opposite / Adjacent

Reciprocals: cosec θ = 1/sin θ, sec θ = 1/cos θ, cot θ = 1/tan θ.

Ratios don't depend on size

Both triangles share the same acute angle θ but differ in size. Change θ and watch: the ratios stay identical, because similar triangles have proportional sides.

Each identity is evaluated numerically from the current θ. The right-hand column is computed from live sin/cos/tan values — it always lands on the expected result.

Facts panel

  • Six ratios (angle θ in a right triangle): sin=opp/hyp, cos=adj/hyp, tan=opp/adj, cosec=hyp/opp, sec=hyp/adj, cot=adj/opp.
  • SOH-CAH-TOA — Sin=Opp/Hyp, Cos=Adj/Hyp, Tan=Opp/Adj.
  • Reciprocal pairs — sin&cosec, cos&sec, tan&cot each multiply to 1.
  • Quotient — tan θ = sin θ / cos θ and cot θ = cos θ / sin θ.
  • Pythagorean identities — sin²θ + cos²θ = 1, 1 + tan²θ = sec²θ, 1 + cot²θ = cosec²θ.
  • Range — for an acute angle, sin θ and cos θ lie between 0 and 1; opposite & adjacent are named relative to θ.

Key Facts

  • Law of conservation of mass: in a chemical reaction, mass is neither created nor destroyed. The total mass of reactants equals the total mass of products.
  • Why balance? Atoms are only rearranged, never lost or made. So the number of atoms of each element must be the same on both sides — that is what a balanced equation shows.
  • A balanced equation also gives the correct ratio in which substances react and are produced.
  • Symbols: (s) solid, (l) liquid, (g) gas, (aq) dissolved in water. An arrow → separates reactants from products.

Neuron

The nerve cell

A neuron is the structural and functional unit of the nervous system. Tap the dendrite, cell body, axon or synapse to learn its job.

Reflex arc: hand off a hot object

Key facts

  • A reflex action is an automatic, rapid response that does not need conscious thought from the brain.
  • The path taken by the nerve impulse in a reflex is the reflex arc, coordinated by the spinal cord.
  • Order: stimulus → receptor → sensory neuron → spinal cord → motor neuron → effector (muscle).

Nervous system

Master control network

The nervous system has two parts. Tap a labelled region of the brain or the spinal cord to learn what it does.

CNS

Central Nervous System = brain + spinal cord. It processes information and makes decisions.

PNS

Peripheral Nervous System = all nerves branching out. It carries messages to and from the CNS.

Key facts

  • The cerebrum is the largest part - the seat of thinking, memory and voluntary action.
  • The cerebellum keeps posture and balance, and coordinates precise muscle movement.
  • The medulla oblongata controls involuntary vital actions: heartbeat, breathing, blood pressure.

Endocrine

Chemical messengers

Endocrine glands release hormones straight into the blood. Tap a gland to see the hormone it makes and what it does.

Nervous control

Uses electrical impulses through nerves. Very fast, short-lived, and targeted.

Hormonal control

Uses chemicals in the blood. Slower to start but longer-lasting and widespread.

Bonus: coordination in plants

Plants have no nervous system. They respond with tropisms - directional growth guided by hormones. Auxin gathers on the shaded side of a shoot, making those cells grow longer so the shoot bends toward light (phototropism). Roots show gravitropism, growing downward.

Key facts

  • The pituitary is the "master gland" - its hormones control other glands and growth.
  • Insulin from the pancreas lowers blood sugar; too little causes diabetes.
  • Adrenaline from adrenal glands prepares the body for "fight or flight" emergencies.

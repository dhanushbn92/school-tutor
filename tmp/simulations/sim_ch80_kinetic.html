The distribution broadens and shifts right as T rises. Average translational KE is ½m⟨v²⟩ = (3/2)kT, so it depends only on temperature — not on the gas. Lighter molecules move faster (larger v_rms).

Key formulas

PV = nRT
Ideal gas equation. R = 8.314 J·mol⁻¹·K⁻¹, n = moles, T in kelvin.
P = ⅓ ρ <v²>
Pressure from kinetic theory; ρ = density, ⟨v²⟩ = mean-square speed.
KE_avg = ½ m <v²> = (3/2) k T
Mean translational KE per molecule. k = 1.38×10⁻²³ J/K (Boltzmann).
v_rms = √(3kT/m) = √(3RT/M)
Root-mean-square speed. M = molar mass (kg/mol). Lighter gas ⇒ faster.
v_mp = √(2kT/m) < v_avg = √(8kT/πm) < v_rms
The three characteristic speeds always keep this order.

यक्ष-युधिष्ठिर संवादः

Yaksha Prashna · The Riddle Game

NIOS Class 10 Sanskrit · Chapter 12 · Mahabharata (Vana Parva)

कथा-सन्दर्भः / The Story: During their forest exile, the five Pandavas grew thirsty. One by one, four brothers went to a lake to fetch water, but a यक्षः (Yaksha) — a nature-spirit guarding the lake — warned them to answer his questions first. They ignored him, drank, and fell lifeless. At last युधिष्ठिरः (Yudhishthira) came, and wisely answered every riddle about धर्मः (dharma). Pleased, the Yaksha (who was really यमधर्मराजः in disguise) revived his brothers. These famous यक्षप्रश्नाः teach timeless wisdom.

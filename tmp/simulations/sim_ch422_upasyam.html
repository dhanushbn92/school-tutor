NIOS Class 12 Sanskrit · Chapter 7 · The Dignity of Labour & Karma-Yoga

कथा · The Two Devotees

A short story on why honest work itself is true worship — then explore the verses that teach it.

एकस्मिन् ग्रामे द्वौ भक्तौ आस्ताम्। प्रथमः दिनरात्रं मन्दिरे उपविश्य केवलं मन्त्रान् अपठत्, कर्म तु न अकरोत्।
ekasmin grāme dvau bhaktau āstām. prathamaḥ dinarātraṁ mandire upaviśya kevalaṁ mantrān apaṭhat, karma tu na akarot.
In a village lived two devotees. The first sat in the temple day and night merely reciting mantras, but did no work.
द्वितीयः कृषकः प्रातः क्षेत्रं गत्वा श्रमेण कृषिम् अकरोत्, अन्नं च दीनेभ्यः अयच्छत्। सः वदति स्म — "मम कर्म एव मम पूजा।"
dvitīyaḥ kṛṣakaḥ prātaḥ kṣetraṁ gatvā śrameṇa kṛṣim akarot, annaṁ ca dīnebhyaḥ ayacchat. saḥ vadati sma — "mama karma eva mama pūjā."
The second was a farmer; at dawn he went to the field, worked hard at farming, and gave grain to the poor. He would say, "My work itself is my worship."
एकदा दुर्भिक्षे प्रथमस्य मन्त्राः न कञ्चित् अरक्षन्, किन्तु कृषकस्य श्रमजातम् अन्नं बहून् जनान् अपालयत्। तदा ग्रामीणाः अवदन् — "यः स्वकर्मणा परान् सेवते, सः एव सत्यम् उपास्यः।"
ekadā durbhikṣe prathamasya mantrāḥ na kañcit arakṣan, kintu kṛṣakasya śramajātam annaṁ bahūn janān apālayat. tadā grāmīṇāḥ avadan — "yaḥ svakarmaṇā parān sevate, saḥ eva satyam upāsyaḥ."
Once, in a famine, the first man's mantras saved no one, but the grain born of the farmer's labour sustained many people. Then the villagers said, "One who serves others through his own work — he alone is truly worthy of honour."

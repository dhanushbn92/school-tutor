Definitions (unit circle)

On a circle of radius 1 centred at the origin, a point at angle θ (measured anticlockwise from the positive x-axis) has coordinates (cos θ, sin θ).

tan θ = sin θ / cos θ — the slope of the radius line. It is undefined when cos θ = 0 (at 90° and 270°).

Radian measure

One radian is the angle subtended at the centre by an arc equal in length to the radius. A full turn = 2π radians = 360°, so π rad = 180°.

Period & amplitude

sin and cos repeat every 360° (2π) — their period is 2π and amplitude is 1, so values stay in [-1, 1]. tan repeats every 180° (π).

Signs by quadrant (ASTC)

All positive in QI, Sin positive in QII, Tan positive in QIII, Cos positive in QIV — remember "All Silver Tea Cups".

Reference angle

The acute angle between the terminal side and the x-axis. Ratios of θ equal those of the reference angle, up to the sign given by ASTC.

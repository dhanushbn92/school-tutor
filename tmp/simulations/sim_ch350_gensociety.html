Facts Panel

  • Humans have 46 chromosomes (23 pairs); 22 autosome pairs + 1 sex-chromosome pair (XX female, XY male).
  • Mendelian / single-gene disorders arise from a mutation in one gene; chromosomal disorders arise from a change in chromosome number or structure.
  • A carrier has one copy of a recessive allele but is usually unaffected; two carriers have a 1 in 4 risk of an affected child.
  • Aneuploidy (an abnormal chromosome number, e.g. trisomy 21) usually results from non-disjunction during meiosis.
  • The Human Genome Project (completed 2003) sequenced the ~3 billion base pairs of human DNA.

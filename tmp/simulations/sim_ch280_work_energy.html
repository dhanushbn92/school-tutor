Key facts

  • Work: W = F d cosθ — force × distance × cosine of angle between them. Unit: joule (J). 1 J = 1 N·m.
  • Work is zero when the force is perpendicular to motion (θ = 90°, cos90° = 0), e.g. carrying a bag horizontally.
  • Kinetic energy: KE = ½ m v² — energy of motion.
  • Potential energy (gravitational): PE = m g h, with g = 9.8 m/s².
  • Conservation of energy: without friction, KE + PE = constant. Energy only changes form.
  • Power: P = W / t = rate of doing work. Also P = F v. Unit: watt (W). 1 W = 1 J/s.

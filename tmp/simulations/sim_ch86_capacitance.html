Formulas

Potential of point charge: V = kQ/r, with k = 1/4πε₀ ≈ 9×10⁹ N·m²/C²
Capacitance definition: C = Q/V
Parallel-plate: C = κε₀A/d, ε₀ = 8.854×10⁻¹² F/m
Energy stored: U = ½CV² = ½QV = Q²/2C
Series: 1/C = 1/C₁ + 1/C₂  ·  Parallel: C = C₁ + C₂

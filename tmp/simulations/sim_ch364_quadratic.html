    Quadratic equation

    • Standard form: ax² + bx + c = 0, with a ≠ 0.
    • Quadratic formula: x = (−b ± √(b²−4ac)) / (2a).

    Discriminant D = b² − 4ac

    • D > 0 → two distinct real roots (parabola cuts x-axis twice).
    • D = 0 → two equal real roots (parabola touches x-axis).
    • D < 0 → no real roots; two complex conjugate roots (no x-intercept).

    Sum & product of roots

    • If roots are α, β then α + β = −b/a and α·β = c/a.
    • Equation from roots: x² − (α+β)x + αβ = 0.

    Vertex & axis

    • Axis of symmetry: x = −b/(2a). Vertex y = c − b²/(4a).
    • a > 0 opens upward (minimum); a < 0 opens downward (maximum).

    Linear inequality rules

    • Add/subtract the same number to both sides — sign unchanged.
    • Multiply/divide by a positive number — sign unchanged.
    • Multiply/divide by a negative number — reverse the sign.
    • Open circle for strict (<, >); closed circle for ≤, ≥.

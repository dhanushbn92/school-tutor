Wave propagation modes

Ground (surface) wave — follows Earth's curvature; used below ~2 MHz (AM broadcast, local range).
Sky wave — reflected by the ionosphere; ~3–30 MHz (short-wave), enables long-distance HF radio.
Space (line-of-sight) wave — travels straight; above ~30 MHz (TV, FM, satellite), needs tall antennas or relays.

Key facts & formulas

Wave speed: c = f λ — c = 3×10⁸ m/s; f in Hz, λ in metres.
Modulation index (AM): ma = Am / Ac; keep ma ≤ 1 to avoid distortion.
AM signal: carrier amplitude varies with the message; three components — carrier + two sidebands.
AM bandwidth: BW = 2 fm (fm = highest message frequency).
FM signal: carrier frequency varies with the message; constant amplitude → better noise immunity, wider bandwidth.
Modulation: impressing a low-frequency message onto a high-frequency carrier for efficient antenna radiation and transmission.
Antenna size: ~ λ/4; high carrier frequency ⇒ short λ ⇒ practical antenna.

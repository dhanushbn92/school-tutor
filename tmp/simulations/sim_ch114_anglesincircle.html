Key facts

  • The angle subtended by an arc at the centre is twice the angle it subtends at any point on the remaining circumference. ∠centre = 2 × ∠circumference.
  • Angles in the same segment of a circle are equal (they subtend the same chord).
  • The angle in a semicircle is a right angle (90°) — Thales' theorem. (Central angle of a diameter = 180°, so inscribed = 90°.)
  • In a cyclic quadrilateral, opposite angles are supplementary: ∠A + ∠C = 180° and ∠B + ∠D = 180°.

Quick check — 3 questions

1. An arc subtends an angle of 80° at the centre of a circle. What angle does it subtend at a point on the remaining circumference?

Angle at circumference = ½ × angle at centre = ½ × 80° = 40°.

2. AB is a diameter of a circle and P is any other point on the circle. What is ∠APB?

The angle in a semicircle is a right angle (Thales' theorem), so ∠APB = 90°.

3. In a cyclic quadrilateral ABCD, ∠A = 70°. What is ∠C?

Opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral are supplementary: ∠C = 180° − 70° = 110°.

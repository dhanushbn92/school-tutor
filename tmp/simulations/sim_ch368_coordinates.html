Formula reference

Distance: d = √[(x₂−x₁)² + (y₂−y₁)²]

Section (internal, ratio m:n): ( (mx₂+nx₁)/(m+n) , (my₂+ny₁)/(m+n) )

Section (external, ratio m:n): ( (mx₂−nx₁)/(m−n) , (my₂−ny₁)/(m−n) )

Midpoint: ( (x₁+x₂)/2 , (y₁+y₂)/2 ) — section with m:n = 1:1.

Area of triangle: ½|x₁(y₂−y₃)+x₂(y₃−y₁)+x₃(y₁−y₂)|

Collinearity: three points are collinear ⇔ area of the triangle = 0.

Centroid: G = ( (x₁+x₂+x₃)/3 , (y₁+y₂+y₃)/3 )

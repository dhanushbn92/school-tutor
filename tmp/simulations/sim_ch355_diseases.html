🦟 Mosquito vector life-cycle (malaria & dengue)

Female Anopheles mosquitoes transmit malaria (Plasmodium); female Aedes aegypti transmit dengue virus. Both breed in stagnant water — the first three stages are aquatic. Control focuses on eliminating breeding sites and preventing bites.

Non-infectious & Lifestyle Diseases

These are non-communicable — not caused by pathogens and not passed person to person. They arise from genetics, diet, and lifestyle.

Communicable (infectious)

  • Caused by pathogens: bacteria, viruses, protozoa, fungi, worms
  • Spread person-to-person or by vectors/water/air
  • Often acute; many are preventable by vaccines/hygiene
  • e.g. TB, cholera, malaria, influenza, COVID-19

Non-communicable (lifestyle)

  • Caused by genetics, diet, inactivity, tobacco, stress
  • Do NOT spread between people
  • Usually chronic, developing over years
  • e.g. diabetes, hypertension, cancer, heart disease

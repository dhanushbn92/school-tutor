Key facts & formulae

  • Laws of reflection: (1) angle of incidence = angle of reflection (∠i = ∠r). (2) The incident ray, reflected ray and the normal all lie in one plane.
  • Mirror formula: 1/f = 1/v + 1/u   Magnification m = -v/u = h'/h. Focal length f = R/2.
  • Lens formula: 1/f = 1/v - 1/u   Magnification m = v/u = h'/h. Power P = 1/f (m) dioptre.
  • Laws of refraction (Snell): n₁ sin i = n₂ sin r. Light bends toward the normal entering a denser medium, away when entering a rarer one.
  • Sign convention (Cartesian): distances measured from the pole/optical centre; those in the direction of incident light are +, opposite are −. Real image distance v is + for lens (behind), −-side for mirror (in front).
  • Concave mirror / convex lens can form real, inverted images. Convex mirror / concave lens always give virtual, erect, diminished images.

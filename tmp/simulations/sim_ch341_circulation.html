Double circulation

Pulmonary circuit: RV → pulmonary artery → lungs → pulmonary vein → LA (picks up O₂).

Systemic circuit: LV → aorta → body → vena cava → RA (delivers O₂).

Blood passes through the heart twice per full loop — oxygenated and deoxygenated blood never mix.

Red Blood Cells (RBC)

Biconcave, no nucleus (mammals). Carry O₂ via haemoglobin; ~5 million/mm³; live ~120 days. Made in red bone marrow.

White Blood Cells (WBC)

Nucleated defence cells; ~7000/mm³. Neutrophils/monocytes phagocytose; lymphocytes make antibodies (immunity).

Platelets (Thrombocytes)

Cell fragments; ~2.5 lakh/mm³. Trigger clotting to seal wounds and stop bleeding.

Plasma

~55% of blood; straw-coloured fluid (90% water). Carries proteins, nutrients, hormones, wastes, CO₂ and heat.

Blood groups & transfusion tool

ABO group = antigen on RBC. Rh⁺ has D-antigen. A safe transfusion means the recipient's plasma antibodies must not attack the donor's RBC antigens.

Blood pressure & pulse

BP is the force of blood on artery walls, written as systolic/diastolic mm Hg. Normal adult ≈ 120/80.

Pulse = the artery expanding with each ventricular systole; resting rate ~72 beats/min.

Key Formulas

Magnetic flux: Φ = B·A·cosθ (weber, Wb)

Faraday's law: ε = −N (dΦ/dt) — the minus sign is Lenz's law.

AC generator EMF: ε = NBAω·sin(ωt), peak ε₀ = NBAω

Angular frequency: ω = 2πf = 2π·(rpm/60)

RMS value: V_rms = V₀/√2 ≈ 0.707 V₀

Ideal transformer: Vp/Vs = Np/Ns = Is/Ip

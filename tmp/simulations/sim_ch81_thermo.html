Key formulas

ΔU = Q − W

First law. W = work done by the gas; Q = heat added to the gas.

W = ∫ P dV

Work done by the gas = area under the process curve on a P–V diagram.

η = 1 − TC / TH

Carnot (maximum) efficiency of a heat engine, temperatures in kelvin.

U = (3/2) n R T

Internal energy of a monatomic ideal gas (R = 8.314 J·mol⁻¹·K⁻¹).

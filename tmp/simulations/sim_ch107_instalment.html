Key facts

The vocabulary of instalment buying.

Cash price

The full price if you pay everything at once, on the spot. This is the cheapest way to buy.

Down payment (cash down)

An amount paid immediately when buying. The rest (cash price − down payment) is the balance financed.

Instalment

Each equal periodic payment (usually monthly) that clears the balance over time.

Interest in instalment schemes

Because you pay over time, the seller charges simple interest on the unpaid balance. So total paid > cash price. The gap is the interest.

Working rule used here: with balance P = cash − down, rate r% p.a., and n equal monthly instalments X:  P(1 + rn/1200) = X·n + X·r·n(n−1)/2400. Each instalment gathers simple interest for the months it stays unpaid.

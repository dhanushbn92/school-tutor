The three coordinate planes split space into 8 octants.

Two points A, B

Vector OP = (a, b, c)

Direction ratios ∝ (a,b,c); direction cosines l=a/r, m=b/r, n=c/r with r=√(a²+b²+c²).

Facts

  • Octants: the 3 coordinate planes divide space into 8 octants by the signs of (x,y,z).
  • Distance: PQ = √[(x₂−x₁)²+(y₂−y₁)²+(z₂−z₁)²].
  • Midpoint: ((x₁+x₂)/2, (y₁+y₂)/2, (z₁+z₂)/2).
  • Section (m:n internal): ((mx₂+nx₁)/(m+n), (my₂+ny₁)/(m+n), (mz₂+nz₁)/(m+n)).
  • Direction cosines: l=cosα, m=cosβ, n=cosγ and l²+m²+n²=1.

Quick Quiz

1. In which octant does the point (−2, 3, −5) lie by signs (x,y,z)?
A) (+,+,+) B) (−,+,−) C) (−,−,−) D) (+,−,+)
Answer: B — signs are (−, +, −).
2. Distance between (1,2,3) and (4,6,15) is?
A) 13 B) 12 C) √13 D) 25
Answer: A — √(3²+4²+12²)=√(9+16+144)=√169=13.
3. If a line has direction cosines l, m, n, then l²+m²+n² equals?
A) 0 B) 3 C) 1 D) depends on the line
Answer: C — always 1.

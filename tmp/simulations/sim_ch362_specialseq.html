Facts Panel

  • Sum of naturals: Σk=1n k = n(n+1)/2
  • Sum of squares: Σk=1n k² = n(n+1)(2n+1)/6
  • Sum of cubes: Σk=1n k³ = [n(n+1)/2]² = (Σk)²
  • Triangular number: Tₙ = n(n+1)/2 = 1, 3, 6, 10, 15, …
  • Square number: n² = 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, …  (also Tₙ + Tₙ₋₁)
  • Fibonacci: 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21, … with Fₙ=Fₙ₋₁+Fₙ₋₂
  • Golden ratio: φ = (1+√5)/2 ≈ 1.618, the limit of Fₙ/Fₙ₋₁

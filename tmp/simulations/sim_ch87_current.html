Key Formulas

Ohm's law:   V = I × R  →  I = V / R Resistance:  R = ρ L / A   (ρ = resistivity, L = length, A = area) Series:      R₋ q = R₁ + R₂  (same current, voltage divides) Parallel:    1/R₋ q = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂  (same voltage, current divides) Power:       P = V I = I² R = V² / R  (watts)

Units: V in volts (V), I in amperes (A), R in ohms (Ω), ρ in Ω·m, P in watts (W).

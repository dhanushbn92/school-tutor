Facts

  • Probability axioms: for any event E, 0 ≤ P(E) ≤ 1; P(S)=1; P(∅)=0.
  • Classical definition: equally likely outcomes → P(E)=n(E)/n(S).
  • Law of large numbers: as trials → ∞, relative frequency → P(E).

Facts

  • Addition rule: P(A∪B) = P(A) + P(B) − P(A∩B).
  • Mutually exclusive (A∩B = ∅): P(A∪B) = P(A) + P(B).
  • Complement: P(A′) = 1 − P(A).

Facts

  • Conditional: P(A|B) = P(A∩B) / P(B), provided P(B) > 0.
  • Multiplication: P(A∩B) = P(B)·P(A|B).
  • Independence: A, B independent ⇔ P(A∩B) = P(A)·P(B), i.e. P(A|B)=P(A).
  • With replacement → draws independent; without replacement → dependent.

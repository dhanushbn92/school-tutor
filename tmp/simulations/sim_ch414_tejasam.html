NIOS Class 10 Sanskrit · Chapter 21 · "For the brilliant, age is not considered"

A young prodigy's tale, then classical verses on why brilliance outshines age.

🌟 कथा · The Boy Sage Ashtavakra (अष्टावक्रः)

एकः बालकः आसीत् अष्टावक्रः नाम। तस्य शरीरम् अष्टसु स्थानेषु वक्रम् आसीत्, परन्तु तस्य बुद्धिः अतीव तीक्ष्णा आसीत्।
ekaḥ bālakaḥ āsīt aṣṭāvakraḥ nāma. tasya śarīram aṣṭasu sthāneṣu vakram āsīt, parantu tasya buddhiḥ atīva tīkṣṇā āsīt.
There was a boy named Ashtavakra. His body was bent in eight places, but his intellect was very sharp.
बालः जनकस्य सभाम् अगच्छत्। विद्वांसः तं दृष्ट्वा अहसन्। बालः अवदत् — "यूयं चर्मकाराः इव केवलं त्वचम् एव पश्यथ, न तु ज्ञानम्!"
bālaḥ janakasya sabhām agacchat. vidvāṁsaḥ taṁ dṛṣṭvā ahasan. bālaḥ avadat — "yūyaṁ carmakārāḥ iva kevalaṁ tvacam eva paśyatha, na tu jñānam!"
The boy went to King Janaka's court. The scholars laughed on seeing him. The boy said — "Like cobblers, you look only at the skin, not at knowledge!"
सर्वे विस्मिताः अभवन्। ते अवदन् — "अयं बालः वयसा लघुः, किन्तु तेजसा महान्।" तेजसां हि न वयः समीक्ष्यते॥
sarve vismitāḥ abhavan. te avadan — "ayaṁ bālaḥ vayasā laghuḥ, kintu tejasā mahān." tejasāṁ hi na vayaḥ samīkṣyate.
All were amazed. They said — "This boy is small in age, but great in brilliance." For the brilliant, indeed, age is not considered.

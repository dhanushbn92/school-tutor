Key facts

  • i² = −1; a complex number is z = a + bi with a = Re(z), b = Im(z).
  • Modulus: |z| = √(a²+b²) — the distance from the origin.
  • Argument: arg(z) = atan2(b, a), the angle from the positive real axis.
  • Conjugate: z̄ = a − bi; reflects z across the real axis, and z·z̄ = |z|².
  • Polar form: z = r(cosθ + i sinθ), where r = |z|, θ = arg(z).
  • Products: moduli multiply, arguments add: |z₁z₂| = |z₁||z₂|, arg(z₁z₂) = arg z₁ + arg z₂.
  • De Moivre: zⁿ = rⁿ(cos nθ + i sin nθ).
  • Roots of unity: solutions of zⁿ = 1 are cos(2πk/n) + i sin(2πk/n), k = 0…n−1 — vertices of a regular n-gon on the unit circle.

Ratio of areas & a shadow application

Facts panel

  • Similar triangles (~): same shape, not necessarily same size. Corresponding angles equal AND corresponding sides in the same ratio k.
  • AA (AAA) similarity: if two angles of one triangle equal two angles of another, the triangles are similar.
  • SSS similarity: if the three pairs of corresponding sides are in the same ratio, the triangles are similar.
  • SAS similarity: if two pairs of sides are in the same ratio and the included angles are equal, the triangles are similar.
  • Basic Proportionality Theorem (Thales): a line parallel to one side of a triangle divides the other two sides in the same ratio — AD/DB = AE/EC.
  • Ratio of areas: for similar triangles, area(△DEF)/area(△ABC) = k² = (ratio of any pair of corresponding sides)².
  • Congruent = special case: congruent triangles are similar with k = 1 (same shape and same size).

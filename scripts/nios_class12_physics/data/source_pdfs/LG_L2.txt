Speed and Velocity 
 The total length of the path 
covered by a body is the distance 
travelled by it. 
 The difference between the initial 
and final position vectors of a 
body is called its displacement. 
 Displacement is the shortest 
distance between the two positions 
and has a certain direction. 
 The rate of change of distance 
with time is called speed 
 The rate of change of displacement 
is known as velocity.

Position - Time Graph 
 The different positions and 
corresponding times can be

time curve. 
  The time is represented 
along x-axis whereas the 
position of the body is 
represented along y-axis.
